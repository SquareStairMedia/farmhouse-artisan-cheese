Back to Busy: Easy Ways to Bring Artisan Cheese Into Weeknight Meals

September has a way of changing the rhythm of the week. Here are a few ways to make busy September evenings feel a little more delicious.

September has a way of changing the rhythm of the week.

Summer schedules give way to school mornings, workdays, after-school activities, appointments, practices, and evenings that seem to fill themselves before dinner has even been considered.

On nights like these, cooking does not have to mean starting from scratch.

A good piece of artisan cheese can turn something very simple into a meal worth looking forward to, while a few thoughtfully chosen ready-made favourites can make getting dinner on the table much easier.

Here are a few ways to make busy September evenings feel a little more delicious.

Make Grilled Cheese Feel Like Dinner

There is nothing wrong with a simple grilled cheese sandwich. But changing the cheese can completely change the meal.

Instead of reaching automatically for the usual choice, try a mature cheddar for more depth, an Alpine-style cheese for a nutty flavour and beautiful melt, or combine two cheeses to get the best of both.

Add caramelized onion, sliced tomato, grainy mustard, or a little chutney if you have it.

Serve it with a bowl of soup or a simple green salad and suddenly grilled cheese is not an emergency dinner. It is dinner.

Farmhouse also makes gourmet grilled cheese sandwiches in the shop for the nights when even assembling the sandwich feels like one job too many.

Give Pasta a Cheese-Counter Upgrade

Pasta is one of the easiest weeknight dinners to put together, and artisan cheese can make even a very simple version feel special.

A good Parmigiano-Reggiano adds savoury depth to tomato sauce, pesto, or buttered pasta. Crumbled blue cheese can transform a creamy pasta sauce. Goat cheese stirred into hot pasta creates an almost instant creamy coating, while an aged cheddar brings plenty of flavour to baked pasta dishes.

The important thing is that you do not need very much.

A strongly flavoured cheese can often do more for a simple pasta dish than a long list of additional ingredients.

And when there simply is not time to make it yourself, Farmhouse’s housemade mac and cheese offers the same kind of comfort with dinner already taken care of.

"A strongly flavoured cheese can often do more for a simple pasta dish than a long list of additional ingredients."

Turn the Cheese Board Into Supper

Cheese boards do not have to be reserved for entertaining.

On particularly busy evenings, they can also be one of the easiest dinners you make.

Choose two or three cheeses and add some bread or crackers, charcuterie, olives, nuts, preserves, and whatever vegetables or fruit happen to be in the kitchen.

There are no rules about making it look elaborate.

For a family supper, put everything in the middle of the table and let everyone build their own plate. It requires very little preparation, creates almost no cleanup, and gives everyone something they like.

It is also a good way to use those small pieces of cheese, half jars of preserves, and other ingredients already waiting in the refrigerator.

Add Better Cheese to Familiar Meals

You do not always need a new recipe.

Sometimes the easiest way to make a weeknight dinner more interesting is simply to improve one ingredient.

Add crumbled goat cheese to roasted vegetables.

Shave an aged hard cheese over salad.

Melt a nutty cheese into an omelette.

Add blue cheese to a burger.

Finish a baked potato with aged cheddar.

Top a bowl of soup with toasted bread and melted cheese.

These are small changes, but they can make an ordinary meal taste completely different without adding much work.

Keep a Few Ready-Made Favourites on Hand

Some evenings are simply too busy for cooking.

That is where having something already prepared becomes useful.

At Farmhouse, our housemade selection includes comforting favourites such as mac and cheese, gourmet grilled cheese, pimento dip, and cocktail crisps. You’ll also find a curated selection of locally sourced provisions including tourtière, sausages, pâté, pot pies, and other items that can help take the pressure off dinner.

Keep something ready for the evening when the schedule runs late, practice goes long, or nobody remembered to make a dinner plan.

Convenience does not have to mean settling for something you do not really want to eat.

Make One Ingredient Work Twice

Another useful weeknight habit is choosing ingredients that can become more than one meal.

An aged cheddar bought for sandwiches can be grated over pasta the following evening.

Goat cheese used in a salad can become part of an omelette or spread onto toast.

A piece of Alpine-style cheese can move from an after-school snack to grilled cheese to a quick cheese board later in the week.

Buying good cheese does not necessarily mean planning an elaborate meal around it. Often, the most practical way to enjoy it is simply to let it become part of several everyday meals.

Have an Easy Supper Formula

When you do not know what to make, it helps to have a simple formula rather than another recipe to follow.

Try:

Something cheesy + something fresh + something warm.

That might mean grilled cheese, tomato salad, and soup.

Or mac and cheese, roasted vegetables, and a simple green salad.

Or a wedge of artisan cheese, good bread, charcuterie, and something from the pantry.

The ingredients can change every week. The idea stays the same.

Dinner does not always need to be complicated to feel thoughtful.

Let September Be a Little Easier

The return to routine can be busy enough without every dinner becoming another project.

A few versatile cheeses, a handful of reliable meal ideas, and something ready to go for the busiest nights can make the week much easier.

Stop by Farmhouse Artisan Cheese in Kerr Village and tell us what your September looks like.

Whether you need a cheese that melts beautifully, something to make a simple dinner more interesting, or a ready-made favourite for a night when cooking is not happening, we are always happy to help you find something that works.
