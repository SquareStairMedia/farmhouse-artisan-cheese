How Much Cheese Per Person? A Simple Guide for Any Gathering

Buy too little and your board looks bare before the main course. Buy too much and you're wondering what to do with half a wheel of Brie on a Tuesday. A few simple guidelines make it easy.

Farmhouse Artisan Cheese · October 3, 2026

The quick answer:

  • Cheese is one of several appetizers: plan on 80 to 85 g per person
  • Cheese board is the main event: plan on 120 to 125 g per person
  • Long gathering where guests graze for hours: plan on 125 to 170 g per person

It's one of the most common entertaining questions, and one of the trickiest to judge. Buy too little and your board looks bare before the main course arrives. Buy too much and you're wondering what to do with half a wheel of Brie on a Tuesday.

The good news is that a few simple guidelines make this one of the easiest parts of hosting.

How much cheese do you need per person?

The right amount depends on how much else you're serving. Here's a simple way to plan:

Type of gathering Cheese per person For 20 guests
Cocktail party with several snacks80 to 85 g1.6 to 1.7 kg
Cheese board as the main event120 to 125 g2.4 to 2.5 kg
Long gathering, such as a holiday party or open house125 to 170 g2.5 to 3.4 kg

Think of these as starting points, not hard rules. If your crowd tends to linger by the cheese board (and let's be honest, most do), it never hurts to round up a little. And if the board is doubling as supper, we have easy ideas for turning a cheese board into dinner.

How many types of cheese should you serve?

Here's something we tell customers in the shop all the time: a board with three to five different cheeses feels more generous and interesting than one big chunk of a single cheese, even when the total weight is the same. Guests love having options, and a mix of textures and flavours makes the whole spread feel more special.

A simple formula for a well-balanced cheese board:

  • One soft cheese: something creamy and spreadable, like Brie or a triple cream.
  • One firm or aged cheese: a nutty aged cheddar, Gouda, or Manchego.
  • One bold or blue cheese: for guests who like a little punch.
  • One local or seasonal pick: a great conversation starter, and a nice nod to what's fresh right now.

This mix means there's something for everyone, whether your guests are seasoned cheese lovers or just starting to explore. Not sure where to begin? Browse our cheese selection for a soft, firm, and bold pick to match each style. And whatever you choose, give it time out of the fridge before serving so every cheese is at its best when guests arrive.

How much should you serve with the cheese?

Cheese rarely stands alone. Crackers, sliced baguette, preserves, nuts, olives, and fresh fruit all round out the flavours and help your cheese go further.

As a general guideline, plan for about the same weight in accompaniments as you have in cheese. That keeps the board balanced and gives guests plenty to pair with each bite. Something warm alongside is always welcome, and our roasted butternut squash and sage with melted Gruyère makes an easy shareable appetizer.

Cheese Quantity FAQ

How much cheese do I need for 10 people?

For appetizers, plan on about 800 to 850 g (about 1.8 to 1.9 pounds). If cheese is the main event, plan on 1.2 to 1.25 kg (about 2.6 to 2.8 pounds).

How many cheeses should be on a cheese board?

Three to five is the sweet spot. It gives guests variety without overwhelming the board or your budget.

Should I buy extra cheese?

Slightly, yes. Time of day, the number of other dishes, and how long guests will stay can all shift these numbers, so rounding up a little is a safe bet.
