# Apple, Aged Cheddar & Caramelized Onion Tart

*\[Hero Image: apple-aged-cheddar-caramelized-onion-tart.webp\]*

A simple savoury tart that brings together sweet Ontario apples, sharp aged cheddar, and rich caramelized onions.

**Serves:** 6  
**Prep time:** 20 minutes  
**Cook time:** 35–40 minutes

## Ingredients

* 1 sheet puff pastry, thawed

* 2 medium apples, thinly sliced

* 1 large yellow onion, thinly sliced

* 1 1/2 cups aged cheddar, grated

* 1 tablespoon butter

* 1 tablespoon olive oil

* 1 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves, plus extra for garnish

* 1 teaspoon grainy Dijon mustard

* 1 egg, beaten

* Salt and freshly ground black pepper

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.

2. Heat the butter and olive oil in a skillet over medium-low heat. Add the sliced onion and a small pinch of salt. Cook slowly for about 15–20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the onions are soft, golden, and caramelized.

3. Place the puff pastry on the prepared baking sheet. Lightly score a border about 1 inch from the edge, being careful not to cut all the way through.

4. Spread the Dijon mustard inside the scored border.

5. Sprinkle about two-thirds of the grated aged cheddar evenly over the pastry.

6. Arrange the caramelized onions and sliced apples over the cheese, slightly overlapping the apple slices.

7. Sprinkle with thyme, black pepper, and the remaining cheddar.

8. Brush the exposed pastry border with the beaten egg.

9. Bake for 20–25 minutes, or until the pastry is puffed and golden and the cheese is melted.

10. Allow the tart to rest for about 5 minutes before slicing. Garnish with a little extra fresh thyme and serve warm.

**Farmhouse tip:** A sharp, nutty aged cheddar works especially well here because it balances the sweetness of the apples and onions without disappearing into the pastry.
