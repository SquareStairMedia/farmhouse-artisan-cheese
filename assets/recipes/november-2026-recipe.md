### **Roasted Potatoes with Melted Raclette & Pickled Onions**

*\[Image: november-recipe.webp\]*

Some cheeses are made for melting, and Raclette may be one of the best.

Rich, savoury, and wonderfully creamy when warm, Raclette turns simple roasted potatoes into the kind of comforting November dish that feels special without being complicated.

### **What You’ll Need**

* 1½ lb small or baby potatoes  
* 6 to 8 oz Raclette cheese, sliced  
* 1 tablespoon olive oil  
* Salt and freshly ground black pepper  
* Cornichons, for serving  
* Pickled onions, for serving  
* Fresh thyme, optional

### **How to Make It**

Preheat your oven to 425°F.

Halve the potatoes and toss them with olive oil, salt, and black pepper.

Arrange them cut-side down on a baking sheet and roast for about 25 to 30 minutes, until golden and tender.

Transfer the potatoes to an oven-safe serving dish and arrange the Raclette slices over the top.

Place under the broiler for 2 to 3 minutes, watching closely, until the cheese is melted and bubbling.

Finish with fresh thyme if you like and serve immediately with cornichons and pickled onions.

Simple, warm, and made for sharing.

### **Why Raclette?**

Raclette is prized for the way it melts. Traditionally, the melted cheese is served over warm potatoes with pickled onions and gherkins, making this an easy way to enjoy the classic combination at home.
