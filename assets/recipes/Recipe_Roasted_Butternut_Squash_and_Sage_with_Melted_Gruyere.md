A Cozy Fall Favorite: Roasted Butternut Squash & Sage with Melted Gruyere

The Recipe: Roasted Butternut Squash & Sage with Melted Gruyere

This dish works beautifully as a shareable appetizer or a cozy side to any fall dinner. It's rustic, warm, and just the right amount of indulgent.

What you'll need:  
• 1 small butternut squash, peeled and cubed  
• Olive oil, salt, and pepper  
• A handful of fresh sage leaves  
• A pinch of cinnamon or nutmeg (optional, but lovely)  
• Gruyere cheese, sliced or torn into pieces  
• A drizzle of maple syrup or brown butter  
• Crusty bread or crostini for serving

How to make it:  
Preheat your oven to 400°F.  
Toss the cubed squash with olive oil, salt, pepper, and a light pinch of cinnamon or nutmeg. Roast for 25–30 minutes, until tender and caramelized at the edges.  
In the last 5 minutes of roasting, scatter a few sage leaves over the squash so they crisp up slightly in the oven.  
Transfer the roasted squash to a small baking dish or cast-iron skillet, and layer the Gruyere pieces over the top.  
Return to the oven for 5–7 minutes, just until the cheese is melted and bubbly.  
Finish with a drizzle of maple syrup or a spoonful of brown butter, and serve warm with crusty bread for scooping.

Why Gruyere?

We love Gruyere here because it melts like a dream—smooth, gooey, and just a little nutty. It doesn't compete with the sweetness of the squash; instead, it wraps around it, creating that perfect creamy-savory balance that makes this dish so comforting.

If you'd like a bolder option, a young Taleggio or even a soft goat cheese would also work wonderfully here, especially if you want something with a bit more tang to contrast the sweetness of the squash.
