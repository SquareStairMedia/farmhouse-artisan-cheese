September has one foot in summer and the other firmly in fall. The days are still warm enough to enjoy outside, Ontario orchards are filling with apples and pears, evenings are becoming cooler, and the return to school after Labour Day brings busy schedules back almost overnight.

September
Cheese Highlight

Red Storm

Intense, nutty and deliciously rich. Complex, refined and beautifully balanced with sweet caramel notes. Red Storm, expertly aged for 18 months, is elegantly adorned in a deep red wax that echoes the twilight skies above the peaks of Snowdonia.

This month’s featured cheese is one that feels especially at home as the seasons begin to change. Its flavour makes it a natural partner for the richer, comforting foods we begin reaching for as fall approaches.

Relish this multi-award-winning vintage Red Leicester in a simple sandwich. Sprinkle it generously in recipes. Adorn your after-dinner cheeseboard with a russet-red wedge as the perfect finishing touch.

Try it on a simple cheese board, melted into a favourite meal, or paired with fresh seasonal ingredients.

September in the Shop

Build an Early-Fall Cheese Board

September cheese boards can be wonderfully simple:

Start with three cheeses: something soft and creamy, something firm and savoury, and one cheese with a little more character.

Add fresh seasonal fruit, a jar of preserves, toasted nuts, and your favourite crackers or bread.

You do not need a complicated board or dozens of ingredients. A few good cheeses and thoughtfully chosen accompaniments are often enough.

Not sure which cheeses to choose? Visit the shop and tell us what you’re serving. We’re always happy to help put together a selection.

Back to Busy

September has a way of changing the household routine almost overnight.

School is back, calendars fill up, evenings get busier, and suddenly the question of what’s for dinner arrives much faster than it did during the slower days of summer.

That is where having something good ready to go can make all the difference.

Stop by Farmhouse and explore our selection of housemade and carefully chosen ready-made favourites. From comforting mac and cheese and gourmet grilled cheese to pot pies, tourtière, sausages, dips, and other specialty foods, there are plenty of ways to put something delicious on the table without starting dinner from scratch.

Perfect for busy school nights, after-school activities, or those evenings when cooking simply isn’t going to happen.
