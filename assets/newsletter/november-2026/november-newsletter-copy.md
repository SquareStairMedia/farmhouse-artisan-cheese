## **November Cheese Highlight: Laguiole AOP**

*\[Image: november-laguiole-cheese-of-the-month.jpg\]*

Say hello to Laguiole, the newest arrival at our cheese counter. It's a traditional French cheese from the Aubrac region in the south of France, and we think you're going to like it.

It's made from raw whole cow's milk, with a firm but supple texture and a rich flavour that lingers. Depending on its age, you might catch notes of fresh hay or toasted hazelnut. It's the kind of cheese that rewards a slow bite.

Traditionally it's served at the end of a meal, but it also earns its spot on a November cheese board. Pair it with rustic bread and a handful of walnuts and you're set.

If you love a cheese with a real sense of place, Laguiole is well worth getting to know.

## **Choosing a Cheese Gift Box They'll Actually Enjoy**

*\[Image: november-gift-box.jpg\]*

The best gifts are the ones you get to enjoy, and a cheese box fits that perfectly. Share it over an evening, bring it to a gathering, or stretch it out over a few days.

A good gift box usually has three or five cheeses that go well together, plus crackers, preserves, or nuts chosen with the recipient in mind. So start with the person.

Do they love to entertain? Are they adventurous eaters, or happiest with the familiar favourites? Would they like something ready to open and share the moment it arrives?

A little thinking up front makes the rest easy. When you're ready to plan, we offer curated and customizable gift boxes, and we're happy to help you put one together.

## **Cozy Fall Meals and Ready-Made Favourites**

*\[Image: november-farmhouse-premade.jpg\]*

Not every November evening needs a plan, and honestly, some of the best meals are the simplest.

That's where our housemade favourites come in. Grab our signature mac and cheese, a gourmet grilled cheese, pimento dip, or cocktail crisps, and dinner is practically done.

We also carry locally sourced provisions like tourtière, sausages, pâté, foie gras, and pot pies, perfect for nights when you want something warm and satisfying without the fuss.

Add a simple salad, some warm bread, a bowl of soup, or a favourite cheese, and you're all set.

This is the time of year when comfort beats complexity. Keep a few favourites on hand and let dinner be easy.
