April
Cheese Highlight

This month’s highlight is Wildwood, a beautifully crafted Ontario cheese with a story rooted in tradition, place, and family.

Wildwood is made by Stonetown Artisan Cheese, a family-run farmstead creamery located in St. Marys, Ontario. The Weber family, originally from Switzerland, brought their deep knowledge of Alpine cheesemaking to Canada and built their dairy and creamery around that same philosophy: working with their own herd, using raw milk, and crafting cheese with patience and precision.

What makes Wildwood particularly special is that it’s truly a farmstead cheese. The milk comes directly from the family’s cows, and the cheese is made on-site without additives, allowing the quality of the milk and the skill of the cheesemaker to shine through.

Inspired by traditional Swiss Appenzeller-style cheeses, Wildwood is carefully aged and washed during maturation, developing its signature amber rind and complex aromatic profile. The curds are even washed during the cheesemaking process itself, contributing to its creamier texture and balanced flavour.

On the palate, it offers a beautiful combination of nutty, savoury, and slightly zesty notes, with a smooth, supple texture that becomes even more expressive at room temperature. It’s a washed rind that remains approachable — elegant, layered, and incredibly versatile.

Wildwood is also a cheese that has gained well-deserved recognition, earning Grand Champion at the SIAL International Cheese Competition — a testament to the craftsmanship behind every wheel.

This is a cheese that reflects both its roots and its place — European tradition, Ontario terroir, and true artisan care — making it a perfect centrepiece for April.

Staff Pick

Raea’s Choice: Le Meadow’s Pantry

This month, Raea is highlighting a beautiful new addition to the shop: Le Meadow's Pantry.

Proudly woman-owned and based in British Columbia, The Meadows Pantry creates artisanal preserves using locally sourced produce that is peeled, chopped, and prepared entirely by hand. Each batch is cooked slowly in traditional copper pans (never more than 12 jars at a time), a method inspired by classic French techniques that prioritize flavour, texture, and care.

What makes this brand particularly special is its commitment to simplicity and integrity. The preserves are lower in sugar, made without added pectin, and allow the natural character of the fruit to shine through, resulting in vibrant, balanced flavours that pair beautifully with cheese.

Raea loves them for their balance and versatility, an easy way to bring brightness, depth, and a sense of intention to any board.

A new arrival in the shop, and one we’re very proud to share.

Did You Know?

Québec’s Quiet Revolution: How La Belle Province Became North America’s Cheese Capital

While France rested on centuries of tradition and the rest of North America was still sorting mild from sharp, something remarkable was taking shape in the rural farmlands of Québec.

Over the past four decades, Québec transformed itself from a province of industrial cheese production into one of the most dynamic and respected cheesemaking regions in the world. Today, with over 700 artisan cheeses crafted by more than 100 fromageries, Québec produces more cheese varieties than any other region in Canada and rivals many European countries in quality and innovation.
