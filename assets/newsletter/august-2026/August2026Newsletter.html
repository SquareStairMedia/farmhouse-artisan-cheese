August is when Ontario really shows off.

Fresh peaches are everywhere, local honey catches the flavours of summer, and bottles of Ontario cider are ready to be opened on the patio. Add the right artisan cheese, and these familiar local foods become something worth slowing down to enjoy.

This month, we’re celebrating the flavours close to home and sharing a few simple ways to bring them together. Whether you’re heading to the cottage, hosting friends in the backyard, or enjoying a quiet summer evening, a few thoughtfully chosen ingredients are all you need.

August
Cheese Highlight

Five Brothers

This month’s featured cheese brings our local theme together perfectly.

Five Brothers is made by Gunn’s Hill Artisan Cheese in Woodstock, Ontario. It is a firm yet supple cow’s milk cheese that brings together some of the best qualities of Gouda and Swiss-style cheeses.

Its smooth texture, savoury flavour, and delicate crystals make it approachable enough for almost any cheese lover while still offering plenty of character. It works beautifully on a cheese board, melts well in sandwiches, and pairs easily with many of Ontario’s seasonal flavours.

Try it with fresh peaches, local honey, apple or onion preserves, roasted nuts, or a glass of dry Ontario cider.

Five Brothers is a wonderful reminder that exceptional artisan cheese can be found close to home.

August in the Shop

Build an Ontario-Inspired Cheese Board

A beautiful summer cheese board does not require a long shopping list.

Begin with three cheeses: something soft and creamy, something firm and savoury, and one cheese with a little more intensity. Add fresh Ontario peaches, a jar of preserves, local honey, crackers, and a handful of nuts.

The fun is in trying different combinations. Creamy cheese softens the sweetness of a ripe peach. Aged cheddar stands up beautifully to maple syrup or apple preserves. Blue cheese becomes surprisingly approachable with a small drizzle of honey.

Not sure where to begin? Visit the shop and tell us what you’re serving. We’re always happy to suggest cheeses, accompaniments, and quantities for your gathering.

Five Local Ontario Foods That Pair Beautifully With Artisan Cheese

Looking Ahead

The final weeks of summer have a way of filling up quickly.

There are cottage visits, family dinners, neighbourhood gatherings, and one last opportunity to enjoy a long evening outdoors. Whether you need a simple selection for two or enough cheese for a full table, we can help make planning easier.

Stop by the shop and tell us where you’re going, how many people you’re serving, and what else is on the menu. We’ll help you choose cheeses that travel well, pair easily, and suit a range of tastes.

As summer begins to wind down, we’ll also be preparing for the richer flavours of early fall. Look for aged cheeses, seasonal preserves, comforting housemade favourites, and plenty of ideas for autumn entertaining.
