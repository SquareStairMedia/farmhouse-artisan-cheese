June
Cheese Highlight

Appenzeller Corsé

This month’s highlight is Appenzeller Corsé, one of Switzerland’s most celebrated cheeses and a favourite among those who appreciate bold, complex flavours.

Produced in the Appenzell region of northeastern Switzerland, this cheese has been crafted for more than 700 years using traditional methods. What makes it truly unique is its secret herbal brine, used to wash the wheels during ageing. While the exact recipe remains closely guarded, it’s this process that gives Appenzeller its distinctive character and depth.

The Corsé version is aged longer than the Classic, developing a firmer texture and a richer flavour profile. Expect notes of roasted nuts, warm butter, herbs, and a savoury finish that lingers beautifully on the palate. Despite its intensity, it remains remarkably balanced and approachable.

This is a cheese that shines on a board, pairs beautifully with charcuterie, and rewards slow enjoyment. If you’re looking to discover a new favourite this summer, Appenzeller Corsé is a wonderful place to start.
