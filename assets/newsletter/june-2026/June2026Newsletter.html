May
Cheese Highlight

Fleur du Maquis

This month’s highlight is Fleur du Maquis, a truly distinctive sheep’s milk cheese from Corsica that brings both character and beauty to the table.

Its name — “flower of the maquis” — comes from the fragrant herbs that cover its rind, a reflection of the wild Corsican landscape where it’s made. Coated in rosemary, juniper berries, and other aromatic botanicals, this cheese is as striking visually as it is in flavour.

Inside, the texture is semi-firm yet tender, with a rich, creamy mouthfeel typical of sheep’s milk. On the palate, it offers a delicate balance of nutty sweetness and savoury depth, enhanced by the subtle infusion of herbs from the rind. Each bite feels layered and expressive, without ever becoming overpowering.

Fleur du Maquis is a natural centrepiece, perfect for spring and early summer boards, where it pairs beautifully with simple accompaniments: fresh bread, olives, light preserves, and a glass of something crisp. A cheese that feels both rustic and elegant, and one that always invites conversation.

Staff Pick

Stéphane’s Choice: Rillettes de Poisson et Crustacés

This month, Stéphane is reaching for something that brings a taste of the French coast to any table: Groix Nature Rillettes.

Crafted in Brittany, France, these artisanal rillettes are made with high-quality fish and simple, carefully selected ingredients. The texture is smooth and spreadable, with a delicate richness that feels both indulgent and refined.

What makes Groix Nature particularly special is its focus on sustainability and craftsmanship. The fish is responsibly sourced, and each recipe is designed to highlight the natural flavour of the ingredients: never overworked, never overly seasoned, just clean, balanced, and deeply satisfying.

Stéphane loves them for their versatility. Served on fresh bread, alongside a cheese board, or as part of a light aperitif, they add something unexpected and elegant, a coastal touch that elevates any spread.

A simple pleasure, done exceptionally well.
