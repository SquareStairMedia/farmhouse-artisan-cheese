Our Top 5 Travel-Ready Cheeses

We've pulled together a small selection of cheeses that are particularly well-suited to weekend getaways. These are the ones that hold up beautifully in a cooler, don't demand constant attention, and taste incredible paired with nothing more than crackers and a view.

Aged Cheddar – Firm, robust, and doesn't mind a bit of warmth  
Alpine-Style – Dense, nutty, perfect for slicing on the dock  
Hard Sheep's Milk – Savory and satisfying, travels like a dream  
Semi-Firm Gouda – Crowd-pleasing and virtually indestructible  
Washed-Rind (carefully wrapped) – For the adventurous traveler with good cooler discipline  
Each one brings something different to the table, and we're happy to help you choose what fits your plans.
