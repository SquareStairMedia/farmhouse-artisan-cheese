Cheese Highlights

This month’s cheese highlight is Laguiole, a true classic from the Aubrac plateau in southern France, a cheese with history, character, and remarkable depth of flavour.

Often described as the most refined expression of Cantal, Laguiole represents the highest-quality, traditional style of this historic cheese family. Made from cow’s milk and crafted using time-honoured mountain methods, it’s essentially Cantal at its very best, more complex, more balanced, and more expressive.

Its texture is firm yet smooth, with a natural elasticity that makes it incredibly satisfying to slice and taste. On the palate, Laguiole offers warm nutty notes, gentle sweetness, and a long, savoury finish that lingers without heaviness or sharpness.

This is a cheese that feels both rustic and elegant, perfect for early spring boards, long lunches, and simple meals where the quality of the product speaks for itself. Beautiful with crusty bread, good butter, cured meats, and light preserves, but just as enjoyable on its own.

For lovers of aged French cheeses, Laguiole is a true benchmark, timeless, comforting, and deeply satisfying.

Laguiole vs. Cantal: What’s the Difference?

Laguiole and Cantal come from the same cheese family and region of France, and they’re made using very similar traditional methods. The difference lies in quality, terroir, and refinement.

Cantal is the broader, more widely produced cheese, rustic, hearty, and comforting, with a range of aging styles and flavour profiles.

Laguiole is considered the highest expression of this tradition. Made in the mountainous Aubrac region using stricter standards and traditional mountain techniques, it represents the most refined version of Cantal-style cheese, more balanced, more complex, and more elegant in flavour.

Think of it this way:

Cantal is the classic. Laguiole is the masterpiece.

Both are beautiful cheeses, but Laguiole is where tradition, craftsmanship, and flavour reach their peak.

Staff Pick

Abby’s Choice - Jewels Under the Kilt

This month, Abby’s pick isn’t a cheese, it’s one of our favourite snack accompaniments: Jewels Under The Kilt - Roasted & Flavoured Tree Nuts and the delicious artisan nut lineup from Jewels Under the Kilt.

These nuts are handcrafted in Fergus, Ontario by founder Elisabeth Burrow, who turned a farm-life passion into a thriving small business rooted in artisan techniques and thoughtful sourcing.

Elisabeth and her partner planted fruit and nut trees on their farm and, through experimentation and curiosity, developed a patented roasting process that uses rich dark Canadian maple syrup as a preservative rather than a sweetener, giving the nuts a crisp exterior and deep natural flavour without artificial additives.

What makes these nuts truly special is that every ingredient is chosen with intention. Elisabeth works with local suppliers she knows personally, ensuring quality at every step. The process celebrates the natural richness of the nuts and local flavours, creating products that are not only delicious but feel distinctly Canadian.

There’s also a deeper purpose behind the business. Jewels Under the Kilt prioritises sustainable living, environmental mindfulness, and family values. The brand was created with future generations in mind, and growing with those principles at its heart.

And the excellence of these nuts hasn’t gone unnoticed: their Salted Walnuts were honoured in the Better Homes & Gardens 2025 Food Awards, celebrating outstanding artisan food products for their flavour and quality.

Whether added to a board for crunch and contrast or enjoyed straight from the bag, these artisan nuts bring flavour and purpose to every bite… and that’s why Abby keeps coming back to them!
