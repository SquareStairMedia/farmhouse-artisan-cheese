Welcome to the December edition of The Farmhouse Gazette.

December is such a special time of year in our shop. The shelves feel a little cozier, the neighbourhood is full of small seasonal moments, and we enjoy seeing all the familiar faces drop in as families and friends prepare for their winter gatherings.

Exciting news everyone... We have a new website! Our new site offers a much easier way to learn more about us and stay connected with what is happening in the shop. Take a look...

Visit the Site

What's New
This Season

Winter Arrivals

New cheeses and pantry items have been coming through the door steadily over the past few weeks. Some are returning favourites, others are small-batch finds that we look forward to sharing each season. If you are putting together a board, hosting friends, or simply bringing home something to enjoy at the end of the week, we are always happy to help you explore what's fresh.

Holiday Giftware & Pantry Items

Around the shop, you will find simple, thoughtful pieces for the home and table. There are handcrafted mugs, linens, crackers, preserves, honeys, and other small touches that pair naturally with the season. Everything is meant to feel welcoming and easy to enjoy.

Gift Boxes

Our gift boxes have become a bit of a tradition for many of our visitors. Each one is put together with a mix of cheeses and pantry items chosen to feel balanced and comforting. They are available in different sizes and are often picked up for hosts, neighbours, and family members. We're glad people find them meaningful.

A neighbourhood moment, a curated experience, and thoughtful details for the season ahead

This Month
in the Shop

A Neighbourhood Moment

Kerr Village in Oakville has a certain charm in December. The lights go up, the sidewalks feel busy in a cheerful way, and this year we're adding something new inside the shop. The Elf on the Shelf has made his first appearance at Farmhouse Artisan Cheese. If you stop by, take a moment to look for him. Finding the elf comes with a small seasonal surprise, and it has already brought a lot of smiles to visitors of all ages.

A Curated Cheese Experience

What we carry at Farmhouse is selected with care. We choose makers who focus on quality and character, and we enjoy helping people find something that suits their tastes. Whether you already know what you love or you prefer to explore each visit, we're here to make the experience enjoyable and unrushed.

Feature
Highlights

Entertaining Made Simple

If you're hosting this month, we can help you bring together a relaxed, inviting board. Many of our customers tell us they enjoy mixing a few favourites with one or two new discoveries. It's an easy way to make gatherings feel special without overthinking the details.

Thoughtful Gifting

Sometimes the best gifts are the ones that feel personal and uncomplicated. A small jar of honey, a beautifully wrapped bar of chocolate, or a curated box with a few favourite cheeses often says more than something grand. We're happy to help you choose whatever feels right for the person you have in mind.
